The idea

Numbers.Patterns.Perspective.

A symbolic reading

Explore patterns in a phone number and the meanings the app assigns to them. Readings are for entertainment; they do not predict events or guarantee outcomes.

Calculated on your device

Phoneology calculates readings on your device. It does not read your contacts.

Choose what you keep

Private mode starts on and prevents new readings from being saved to History. It does not erase readings you saved before.
