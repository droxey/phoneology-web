Support

Help with Phoneology’s current development build.

Contact support

Email support@phoneology.app for help with the app, a purchase, or a privacy or deletion request. Start with what happened, your app version, and your device model. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment details, a birthdate, or a complete phone number.

Understand a reading

A reading is a symbolic interpretation for entertainment and reflection. It cannot tell you what will happen or improve your chances of love, income, career success, or good luck. An optional birthdate is used for a separate Life Path calculation.

Keep or remove a reading

Private mode is on by default and prevents new readings from being saved to History. It does not erase earlier saves. Delete individual saved readings from History, or see Delete data for the app’s wider removal controls.

Use iCloud sync

Sync is optional and starts off. A setting change takes effect on the next app launch. Supported saved data may sync through iCloud, including saved phone numbers and timestamps. Cross-device behavior is still being verified. Turning sync off does not delete existing copies.

Purchases and refunds

Phoneology is in development. Report purchasing and recovery are being prepared for release. This website does not offer a paid report. Final availability, pricing, and recovery requirements will be confirmed before release.

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. To request a refund for an Apple purchase, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple determines eligibility. A refund request and deletion of app data are separate actions. See Apple’s refund guidance.

Report a non-sensitive bug

You can report a general bug on GitHub. GitHub issues are public. Do not post personal information, receipts, or account details. Use email for private requests.
