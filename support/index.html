Support

A quick guide to Phoneology’s current development build.

What does a reading mean?

Phoneology uses Western numerology to offer symbolic interpretations of phone numbers. For entertainment. Numerology is not predictive. Readings do not guarantee outcomes.

An optional birthdate is used for a separate Life Path calculation.

How does History work?

Private mode is on by default. While it is on, new readings are not saved to History. It does not erase readings you saved earlier.

Saved readings are stored on your device, with optional iCloud sync. You can delete individual readings from History.

Can I sync or remove my data?

Optional iCloud sync is off by default. Changing the sync setting takes effect the next time you launch the app. Supported saved data, including a saved phone number and timestamp, may sync. Cross-device behavior is still being verified during development.

To clear the app’s current local data, open Settings → Delete all data → Delete. Read the in-app confirmation first. This clears accessible saved readings, the birthdate and preferences, and local drafts and previews. You can also clear the birthdate separately in Settings.

Deletion is blocked while a sync operation is in progress. It can also be blocked if storage is inaccessible or permission is denied. With sync enabled, deletions may propagate or remote records may be imported later. Turning sync off keeps existing copies and does not erase other devices. Exports, backups, and App Store purchases are unchanged.

Read the data deletion guide for the current controls and their limits.

Are reports and purchases available?

Per-number report purchases, ownership lookup, and report restoration are not available. Legacy Pro purchase handling exists separately. Paid features and pricing are not announced here.

How do I get help?

For help or to report a problem, open an issue on Phoneology’s public support tracker. A GitHub account is required to post. Do not include phone numbers, birthdates, purchase receipts, or other personal information. Issues are public.
